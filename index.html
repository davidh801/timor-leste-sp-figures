Figure: Citizen allocations vs government spending, aggregated

Four-bucket comparison. Citizen R1 aggregate share of the $400 budget vs the government R4-implied share of the per-household basket.

Government puts 87% of its per-household basket into veterans; citizens allocate 7%. Citizens direct 22% to categories with no formal scheme cited in R4 (Family, HH w/ sick, Youth w/ siblings, Social-security fam).

Figure: Citizen allocations vs government per-household spending, by category

Per-household detail across the 15 recipient categories. Red bars = citizen R1 mean allocation (share × $400 budget); grey bars = government monthly payment for that category per the R4 script.

Government payments for the three veteran categories are $425 per household; citizens allocate roughly $8 to $11 to each. Citizens allocate more than the government pays for pregnant single ($56 vs $15), single disabled ($52 vs $30), and single mother ($39 vs $20). Elderly categories are roughly matched at $30 to $35 either side. Error bars are 95% CIs on the citizen mean (n = 267 with complete R1 data).
